About Rejuvenate

A ladies-only beauty and aesthetics salon in Dubai Sports City, created for women who want expert care, honest guidance and a calm space to feel renewed.

The Rejuvenate philosophy

Beauty that starts with understanding you

Rejuvenate was created as a private retreat from the rush of everyday life. Every appointment begins with listening, so we can understand your skin, your concerns and the results you are hoping for before recommending any treatment.

Our team combines advanced skincare treatments, professional products and a gentle, considered approach. The goal is not to change how you look, but to help your skin feel healthier, fresher and more confident in a way that still feels like you.
